Our Heritage

More Than Fireworks.
We Light Traditions.

Since 1974, the Mutha family has been synonymous with quality fireworks in Pimpri-Chinchwad. What began as a small neighbourhood store has grown into one of the region's most respected wholesale and retail fireworks destinations — built entirely on community trust.

We source exclusively from certified manufacturers, ensuring every product that leaves our shelves meets safety standards without compromising on the brilliance your celebration deserves.
